Banna FAQ: Hours, Prices, Parking & Delivery

Banna is open daily 11am–2am at 9560 Skillman St #120, Dallas, TX 75243; most main dishes run $17–$29 and takeout, delivery and catering are all available. Hours, vegan options, ordering and what to try on a first visit. Anything else, call us at (214) 758-0752.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of food does Banna serve?

▾

Authentic Eritrean and Ethiopian cuisine, injera, tibs, wats, combo platters, and traditional coffee.

Does Banna have vegetarian or vegan options?

▾

Yes. Eritrean and Ethiopian cuisine has deep vegetarian traditions, our Veggie Special Combo and Beyaynetu platter are both fully vegan.

Do you offer takeout and delivery?

▾

Yes, order directly through our online menu, or through Uber Eats and DoorDash.

What are Banna's hours?

▾

Open daily from 11am to 2am.

Where is Banna located?

▾

9560 Skillman St #120, Dallas, TX 75243.

Do I need a reservation?

▾

Walk-ins are always welcome. Reservations are recommended during peak hours.

How much does a meal at Banna cost?

▾

Most main dishes are $17–$29, sharing combo platters are $24–$28, and appetizers start at $6.75.

Is there parking at Banna?

▾

Yes. Free parking is available in the shopping center lot at 9560 Skillman St #120, Dallas, TX 75243.

Do you cater large groups or events?

▾

Yes. We prepare large trays and group orders for parties, offices and events. Call (214) 758-0752 to arrange it.

How long does takeout take?

▾

Most takeout orders are ready in about 15–25 minutes. Order online or call ahead and we will have it waiting.

Does Banna have a full bar?

▾

Yes. Banna is a restaurant and bar serving Eritrean beer, Tej honey wine and traditional Ethiopian coffee alongside the menu.

Is Banna good for first-timers?

▾

Very. Ask for a combo platter, it lets everyone at the table try tibs, wats and vegan dishes on one shared injera.
