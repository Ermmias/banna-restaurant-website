13. Compliance and restrictions

Not a regulated sector — a restaurant and bar. The one sensitivity is alcohol: we hold a bar licence and Meta restricts alcohol advertising, so please keep alcohol out of the ad's offer and creative focus, and apply any required age/location restrictions if bar content is used at all.

Please avoid: health or dietary claims (no "healthiest", no weight-loss framing); "gluten-free" as an unqualified claim — injera is teff-based but our kitchen is not a certified gluten-free facility; and no claims about being the "#1" or "best" Ethiopian restaurant in Dallas that we can't substantiate.

Please get right: we are Eritrean and Ethiopian — both, in that order. Don't describe us as only Ethiopian, and spell dish names correctly (injera, berbere, tibs, wat, Beyaynetu, Doro Wat, Tej).

Our audience includes Orthodox Christian and Muslim diners — please keep creative respectful of fasting periods and don't build campaigns around pork.
